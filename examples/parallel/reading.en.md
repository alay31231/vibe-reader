# Reading together

[001] A useful reading tool helps us return to a passage after the conversation has moved on.

[002] Keep a link to the sentence that prompted your question. The next reader can follow the link and consider the same words.

[003] A translation can make a difficult paragraph easier to discuss. Keep the original close by so that both versions remain available.
